a letter, just for you

My Lakshmi,
my light.

My Pari,

Diwali has always reminded me of home — the warmth, the lights, the beautiful chaos, the little traditions, and the feeling that everything becomes a little more special when shared with the people we love.

And somewhere along the way, you became part of that feeling for me.

They say Goddess Lakshmi brings prosperity, abundance and positivity into a home. I know the divine can never be compared to anything ordinary, but you have brought your own beautiful kind of abundance into my life: happiness in little moments, courage on difficult days, warmth when life feels cold, and a thousand reasons to look forward to tomorrow.

Because of you, every festival feels a little more like home. Not because of the decorations or the lights, but because you're the person I want to share them with. I find myself looking forward to the smallest things — a celebration, a quiet evening, a silly conversation, or simply being beside you.

I hope you never stop chasing your dreams. I hope you always make room for your own happiness, never doubt the person you're becoming, and remember that you don't have to be strong every single day to be extraordinary.

This Diwali, I wish for your life to be as radiant as a sky full of fireworks, as peaceful as a little diya glowing in the night, and as beautiful as every dream your heart holds.

May the world give back to you all the light you so effortlessly bring into mine.

Happy Diwali, my Lakshmi. ♡

With all my love,
always yours.
